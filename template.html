Keyboard shortcuts
Moving between pages
1–6the pages in the menu, in order: Squad, Analysis, Development, Research, Players, Staff. 5 and 6 open on Last import; press again for the next sub-tab
A GAttributes (the attribute weights) and the Guide
?open or close this list
In a table
← ↑ ↓ →move the marked cell
Enteropen the marked player's profile (so does clicking his name or double-clicking his row); Esc closes it
Ssort by the marked column, again to reverse (or click its heading)
Ccopy the marked player's name for FM's search (Ctrl+C still copies text you've selected)
Xadd the marked player to Players › Compare, or take him out (two at most); so does the icon by his name
Search box
/ or Fgo to it; Enter or Esc to come back
Escempty it

Find a player or club by typing part of the name, or test any column: age<=21 · best=DC · best!=GK · vs>=90 · foot=left · club:levski (contains) · wage<=10k · value<=500k (the top of FM's range) · loan-in, loan-out, trial. Several at once, with spaces: age<=23 best=ST vs>=90.
